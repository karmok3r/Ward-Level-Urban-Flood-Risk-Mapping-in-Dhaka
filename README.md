## 📌 Project Overview
Rapid urbanization and inadequate drainage make Dhaka highly vulnerable to pluvial and fluvial flooding. This project performs ward-level urban flood risk mapping by integrating flood hazard indicators with exposure and vulnerability data. The analysis focuses on identifying high-risk wards to support evidence-based urban flood management.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify flood-prone wards based on topography, rainfall, and drainage characteristics  
- Assess exposure of population and built-up areas to flooding  
- Produce a ward-level flood risk index for decision support  

---

## 🛠 Tools and Technologies
- **QGIS** for spatial data processing, mapping, and visualization  
- **Python** for data preprocessing, normalization, and index calculation  
- **Open DEM, rainfall, land-use, and census data** for flood hazard and exposure analysis  
- **GIS-based weighted overlay / MCDA** for flood risk indexing  

---

## 📊 Methodology
1. Prepare flood hazard layers (elevation, slope, rainfall intensity, drainage density)  
2. Extract exposure indicators (population density, built-up areas)  
3. Normalize and weight indicators to generate a ward-level flood risk index  
4. Classify wards into low, medium, and high flood risk zones

## 📈 Expected Outputs
- Ward-level urban flood risk map of Dhaka  
- Ranked list of high-risk wards  
- Clear visual outputs suitable for planning and academic portfolios  

---

## 🌍 Significance
This small-scale, data-driven project demonstrates practical flood risk assessment at the urban governance level and is directly relevant for climate adaptation planning in Bangladesh. The framework is transferable to other cities facing rapid urban flooding challenges.
